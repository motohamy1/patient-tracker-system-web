Non-negotiable settings and why — do not "simplify" them:
- `reducedMotion: 'reduce'` globally. The Playwright default is `'no-preference'`, which lets the CI runner's OS setting leak into baselines.

- `scale: 'css'` (default) so hi-dpi runners do not double baseline resolution.
- `locale` and `timezoneId` pinned, because they affect number/date formatting and therefore layout.
- No `maxDiffPixels` / `maxDiffPixelRatio` yet: start strict. Relax only with a documented, measured reason.
