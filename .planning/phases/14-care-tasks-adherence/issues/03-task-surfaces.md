# 14-03: Care-task surfaces — subtle by construction

**What to build:** Two calm surfaces: the patient's Home gains a Care tasks region where a due task is one tap away from done and the feedback is a quiet completion state (never a score, never a streak, never a celebration), and the provider's patient Overview gains a Care tasks region showing each active task with its completion history and an assign control.

**Blocked by:** 14-02 (the actions)

**Status:** resolved

- [ ] Patient Home region: tasks assigned to this patient, due-today first, each with a "Mark done" control; the completion state is the task's quiet status line ("Logged 3 times · last on 12 Oct") — no points, levels, streaks, progress scores, fireworks or congratulatory copy (§27, TASK-03)
- [ ] A completed-today task reads as done through type and a calm success-toned pill — not through animation, colour noise or reward language
- [ ] Provider Overview region: active tasks with their completion counts and last-completion dates, the due signal where a due date has passed, and the assign/archive controls for clinical authors
- [ ] Both regions degrade on their own (a failed read empties that region only, §24); both are honest when empty ("No care tasks yet" / "Assign a small task such as a weekly weight")
- [ ] Copy discipline throughout: the verb is the label ("Mark done", "Assign a task"), and no copy manufactures urgency or praise
- [ ] Mobile: single column, 44px targets, the same controls (§26)

**Gate evidence at close:** TASK-01/02 (both surfaces) · TASK-03 (no gaming patterns anywhere) · §24 · §26

## Notes

- The adherence display is deliberately near-absent: a count and a date. The phase's whole risk is drift toward a fitness-app aesthetic, so the surface is checked line by line against §27 at the phase gate.
